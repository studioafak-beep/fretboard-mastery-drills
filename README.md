# 🎼 Resonate: The Interval Intuition Builder

> **Turn your instrument into a compass, not a map.**  
> *Resonate* is a companion tool for musicians who want to stop *looking* for chords and start *feeling* them—by training your ear, your muscle memory, and your mental model of the fretboard or keyboard, all in one focused practice loop.

---

## 🧭 Why Another Chord Trainer?

Most chord apps show you a diagram when you're stuck—which is like giving someone the answer before they've attempted the puzzle. *Resonate* takes the opposite approach: it presents you with a **sonic target**, and your job is to find it on your instrument *before* any visual aid appears. The result? A deep, lasting, and tactile understanding of harmony that survives the transition from practice room to stage.

This is not a reference library. This is a **training ground**—a place where your fingers and ears develop a private language that doesn't require a screen to translate.

---

## ✨ Core Philosophy: The "Echo-Locate" Method

We built *Resonate* around a simple but powerful principle we call **Echo-Location**:

1. **Listen** – A chord rings out, but you don't see its name or shape.  
2. **Hunt** – You must locate that chord across the entire fretboard or keyboard—starting from any position, in any inversion.  
3. **Confirm** – The app listens (via your device's mic or a midi input) and tells you whether you've found a valid voicing, or one that's close but not quite right.  
4. **Reflect** – After the attempt, *then* the visual diagram and chord name are revealed, along with alternative voicings you might have missed.

This cycle builds a *spatial map* of harmony that traditional flashcards or static diagrams cannot provide. You'll stop thinking in shapes and start thinking in **intervals**—the true building blocks of musical language.

## 🎯 Key Features (Beyond the Basics)

### 🔍 "Mist-Finder" Feedback Engine
Instead of a simple "correct/incorrect," *Resonate* tells you *how* you were wrong. Did you play the right notes but in a wrong order? Did you nail the root but miss the third? The engine provides granular, color-coded feedback directly on your screen—so you learn from *why* a voicing failed, not just that it did.

### 🧩 "Interval Chain" Challenge Mode
A gamified progression where each chord you find unlocks the next, but with a twist: the new chord shares only one note with the previous one. This forces you to pivot your thinking by a single interval, dramatically accelerating your ability to find related chords in real time.

### 🌍 Multilingual Ear-Training Library
The terminology isn't locked to English. Switch between German, French, Spanish, Japanese, and simplified Chinese to learn the international names for intervals and chord qualities—a crucial skill for reading charts, jamming with global players, or studying music theory in its original language.

### 📱 Responsive Practice Canvas
The interface adapts not just to different screen sizes, but to different *practice postures*. On a phone, you get a "quick fire" mode for 1-2 minute drills between tasks. On a tablet, you get a full fretboard/keyboard overlay with multiple touch points. On a desktop, you can connect a MIDI controller for the most precise feedback loop.

### 🕒 24/7 Adaptive Practice Sessions
The app learns your weak spots over time. If you consistently miss minor 7th shapes in the key of Ab, the algorithm will begin to feature them more prominently—but only for *your* account. No two users will see the exact same practice path, making every session feel uniquely tailored.

### 🎧 "Hands-Free" Audio Cues
Activate the "audio-locate" mode, and *Resonate* will *speak* the intervals to you through your headphones using a human-like voice, while you keep your eyes on your instrument. This allows for a truly eyes-closed, focused practice session that's ideal for building stage confidence.

## 🧠 Understanding Your Progress (Not Just Stats)

We avoid meaningless "streaks" and "points." Instead, the dashboard tracks **"Resonance Score"**—a composite metric that balances three things:

- **Accuracy** (Did you find the chord?)
- **Efficiency** (How much of the instrument did you search before finding it?)
- **Versatility** (How many *different* voicings did you discover for the same chord before moving on?)

This honest triple-feedback loop gives you a real measure of your growth, not a dopamine hit from a number going up.

## 🚀 Getting Started (Your First Resonance)

1. **Choose Your Canvas** – Select "Guitar," "Bass," "Piano," or "Ukulele" from the opening menu. We support 6-string, 4-string, and 88-key standard tunings, with the option to create custom tuning configurations.
2. **Set Your Level** – From "Novice" (root position, basic triads) to "Virtuoso" (tenths, altered dominants, and drop-2 voicings across the entire neck).
3. **Start the Loop** – The app will play a chord. Your only job is to find it. The internal "Mist-Finder" calibrates to your instrument's tuning and your device's microphone sensitivity in under ten seconds.
4. **The Reveal** – After you confirm your attempt, the visual diagram appears. Study it, compare it to your finger placement, and then press "Try Alternate" to see up to six other valid voicings for that same chord.

There is no "wrong way" to practice. *Resonate* is designed to be flexible enough for a 5-minute warm-up or a 2-hour deep dive.

## 🧪 The Science of "Tactile Theory"

Traditional theory education is a top-down process (learn the rules, then apply them). *Resonate* operates bottom-up. You first *feel* the chord, then the theory is revealed as an *explanation* for what you just did. This experiential approach is widely recognized as a faster and more durable way to form long-term memory for complex motor tasks, which is exactly what playing an instrument is.

Our "Echo-Locate" method is based on the principle of **interleaved practice**—mixing different chord types and keys in a single session to improve your brain's ability to *discriminate* between them. You'll often be surprised by how quickly your hands "know" the answer before your conscious brain does.

## 📚 Supported Chord Families

- **Triads**: Major, Minor, Diminished, Augmented, Suspended 2 & 4
- **Sevenths**: Major 7, Minor 7, Dominant 7, Half-Diminished, Diminished 7, Minor-Major 7
- **Extensions**: 9ths, 11ths, and 13ths (with alteration options like b9, #9, #11, b13)
- **Inversions**: All chord types can be practiced in any inversion, from root position to third inversion across multiple octaves.
- **Drop Voicings** (Guitar/Bass): Drop 2 and Drop 3 voicings are included, a critical skill for jazz and modern harmony.

## ⚙️ Device & Platform Compatibility

*Resonate* is built as a web application, meaning it works instantly on any modern device with a browser and a microphone. No external software or hardware is required, though a MIDI interface is supported for the most precise "confirm" step.

- **Browsers**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest two versions).
- **Audio Input**: Built-in microphone, USB microphone, or any class-compliant MIDI device.
- **Offline Mode**: Once loaded, the core training loop works offline, making it perfect for a plane ride or a studio without Wi-Fi.

---

## 🛠️ Customization & Accessibility

We believe practice tools should adapt to the user, not the other way around.

- **Colorblind-Friendly Visuals**: The feedback color scheme (green/red/amber) can be switched to a high-contrast pattern-based scheme.
- **Left-Handed Mode**: Flips the visual fretboard/keyboard orientation entirely.
- **Custom Tuning**: Create a tuning profile for any stringed instrument (e.g., Drop D, Open G, or a 7-string setup).
- **Volume Compensation**: The app can auto-calibrate its listening threshold to work in noisy environments, so you can practice in a busy living room or a quiet studio.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

*Resonate* is a training aid, not a substitute for a qualified music teacher or a formal ear-training program. While the app is designed to be highly effective for self-led practice, progress depends on consistent effort and the quality of your practice environment. Audio recognition is based on the sound of *your* instrument; environmental noise or improper instrument tuning may affect the feedback accuracy. Please ensure your instrument is tuned and your environment is reasonably quiet for best results. The app does not provide musical notation for songs—it is purely a chord-location and interval-training utility.
